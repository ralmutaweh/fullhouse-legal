Fullhouse is a hospitality operations system for restaurants, cafés, caterers and pop-up venues ("Venues"). It is provided by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], a business registered in the Kingdom of Bahrain under commercial registration [CR NUMBER] ("Fullhouse", "we", "us").

This policy explains what personal data the Fullhouse apps and services process, why, where it is stored and the rights people have over it. It is written with the Kingdom of Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 30 of 2018, the "PDPL") in mind.

1. Our role

Fullhouse is used by Venues to run their business. The role we play depends on the data:

  • Guest data (people who order, book or join a waitlist at a Venue): the Venue decides why and how it is collected and is the data controller. Fullhouse processes it only on the Venue's instructions, as a data processor, under a Data Processing Agreement with the Venue.
  • Account data (the Venue's administrators and staff who use Fullhouse): Fullhouse processes it to provide and secure the service. For this data we act together with the Venue that created the account.

Guests who want to exercise their rights should contact the Venue first. We will help the Venue respond.

Allergens

Allergen information is health-related, which the PDPL treats as sensitive personal data. It is recorded only when a guest tells the Venue about it, with the guest's consent, and is used only to prepare food safely and to warn staff. A guest can ask the Venue at any time to remove it.

3. Legal basis

Personal data is processed where it is needed to perform the Venue's service to the guest or the contract between the Venue and Fullhouse, to meet legal obligations (for example tax invoicing and record keeping), for legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention, or with consent, which is always the basis for allergen information.

4. Where the data is stored

  • On-site installations: some Venues run Fullhouse on a local server (the "Coordinator") at the venue. Their guest and operational data stays on that local server and is not sent to the Fullhouse cloud.
  • Cloud installations: for other Venues, data is stored on servers operated for Fullhouse by our hosting provider, in [HOSTING REGION].
  • On the iPads themselves, the apps keep only what they need to work (for example the sign-in session and recently loaded screens).

5. Who it is shared with

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising. It is shared only with:

  • the Venue that the data belongs to, and the staff it authorises;
  • service providers that help us run Fullhouse (sub-processors), under written obligations to protect it. The current list is: [SUB-PROCESSORS, e.g. hosting provider, network/security provider, email delivery, error monitoring];
  • authorities, where the law requires it.

If data must be transferred outside Bahrain, we do so only as the PDPL allows, for example to countries the Personal Data Protection Authority considers adequately protected.

6. How long it is kept

  • Guest and operational data is kept for as long as the Venue uses Fullhouse, unless the Venue deletes it sooner.
  • Invoices and transaction records are kept for the period required by Bahraini tax and commercial law.
  • Technical logs and bug reports are kept for [RETENTION PERIOD].
  • When a Venue's contract ends, its data is returned or deleted as agreed in the Data Processing Agreement.

7. How it is protected

  • Passwords and PINs are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Each person sees only what their role allows, and branch staff can reach only their own branch.
  • Sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and sessions expire and can be revoked, for example when a device is removed.
  • Cloud traffic is encrypted in transit. On-site installations run on the Venue's private network.
  • Sensitive actions such as voids and refunds are recorded in an audit log.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPL you may ask to be told whether your data is processed, to get a copy of it, to correct it, to have it deleted or blocked where the law allows, and to object to its processing. You may also withdraw consent, for example for allergen information. Guests should contact the Venue; staff should contact their employer. You can also contact us at the address below.

If you are not satisfied with the answer, you may complain to Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Authority.

9. Data breaches

If a breach affects personal data, we inform the affected Venue without undue delay so it can meet its obligations, including notifying the Personal Data Protection Authority where required.

10. Children

Fullhouse is a business tool used by Venue staff. It is not directed at children.
